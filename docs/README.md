# Awesome-Shiksha

I'm creating this page to dump all of my ideas about how an education system shall be and what it should be covered with teaching methodology. Feel free to read it, implement it with your known ones, and reach out to discuss anything relevant! 

The purpose creating this page is to have accessible education for all with access to internet in proper consolidated way. The basic idea, in current education system, of having "board" exam in grade 10 is to check if student is ready to deep dive in specific studies. In other words, Do they have bare minimum knowledge to survive in real world, tackle real problems (not finding innovative solutions, just tackling problems only), and at least knows what different options they have to choose from! But I feel from current system most of the anaswer from above questions will be no even for grade 10 topper. and failure in grade 10 would have better exposure to real life problems. Mostly students still feel clueless in thier real life even after completing their undergrad level studies and don't know what do they want from their life. It's still okay to not knowing what do you want and you're figuring out by experimenting different stuffs that's actually better then following conventional ways of 9-5 jobs when you're not enjoying but staying still and having stagnant life and too much free time is where it all problems start: for individual, for their families, and for a society. So, I wanted to design an educational plan / informal curriculum that covers most of the basics that I feel if I would have known earlier, I would be the different person, not as an academic / career wise but as an human!

- A plan that not only makes an individual a self reliant but improves overall us a society, as a nation.

- A structure that can be used by any parents to homeschool their kids or for any eacher if they want to go extra mile for their students from conventional curriculum.

- A methodology that develops scientific temparament, curious minds, respects new ideas including the ones that may not lie withing the same ideology, and rational thinking.

## How to use this project!
- This project is designed in modular way!


### Raise a Issue for new feature / content
- If you could not find what you're looking for: Raise a [issues](https://github.com/EkPratishat/Awesome-Shiksha/issues) with as detailed explanation and we'll try to add it as soon as possible! Follow below template to provide information: 
    - Target grade / Age group
    - Subject
    - Topic
    - [ Optional ] Suggested methodology
